車輛行車管理系統（含車輛監控系統，Vehicle Traffic Management System, incl. Vehicle
Monitoring System）為全場域營運之核心監控與管理平台，負責彙整場域內車輛、設施及事件
資訊，提供營運人員全景監控與集中管理介面；並依營運需求制定班表、調度及速限等營運策略，
將策略結果交付車輛巡航管理系統（中心端）執行，同時管理載具、場域、服務、媒體、權限與
日誌等營運資源。

The Vehicle Traffic Management System (including the Vehicle Monitoring System) serves as
the core operational monitoring and management platform for the entire field. It aggregates
information on vehicles, facilities and events within the field and provides operators with a
panoramic monitoring and centralized management interface. Based on operational requirements,
it formulates operating strategies such as schedules, dispatching and speed limits, and delivers
the resulting strategies to the Vehicle Cruise Management System (Center Side) for execution.
It also manages operational resources including vehicles, field data, services, media, permissions
and logs.

## 系統邊界與範圍界定 System Scope and Boundary Definition

### 車輛行車管理（含車輛監控系統）負責範圍

1. 提供全場域營運核心監控，包含營運全景圖台、載具監控數據、即時班表資訊及即時營運數據之
   顯示與視覺化。
2. 制定營運策略並產生營運班表，包含時間模板、路線群組、整備任務之設定，以及班表之規劃、
   可行性檢核、部署與清單管理。
3. 執行營運控制，包含班表部署管理、降級運轉管理、派遣調度管理及車門月台控制。
4. 集中管理場域事件，包含事件即時清單、歷史事件檢視、事件報表輸出及影像監控管理。
5. 管理營運資源，包含載具註冊與運行紀錄、場域圖資與站點設施資訊、服務路線與訂單、以及
   媒體資訊。
6. 管理系統後勤，包含帳號與角色權限、系統操作與營運日誌、系統參數設定及系統監測狀態。

1. Provides core operational monitoring of the entire field, including the operation panorama
   dashboard, vehicle monitoring data, real-time schedule information, and real-time operational
   data display and visualization.
2. Formulates operating strategies and generates operational schedules, including the configuration
   of time templates, route groups and maintenance tasks, as well as schedule planning, feasibility
   verification, deployment and list management.
3. Performs operational control, including schedule deployment management, degraded operation
   management, dispatch management, and vehicle and platform door control.
4. Centrally manages field events, including the event information list, historical event review,
   event report generation and video monitoring management.
5. Manages operational resources, including vehicle registration and operation records, field map
   data and station/facility information, service routes and orders, and media information.
6. Manages system logistics, including account and role permissions, system operation and
   operational logs, system parameter settings and system monitoring status.

### 與相鄰子系統分界 Boundaries with Adjacent Subsystems

**車輛巡航管理系統（中心端）Vehicle Cruise Management System (Center Side)**

- 車輛行車管理（含車輛監控系統）：負責車隊營運管理、任務排程、調度規劃及速度限制等營運
  策略之制定，並將策略結果交付巡航管理系統（中心端），**不負責巡航控制邏輯**。
- 車輛巡航管理系統（中心端）：負責接收與解析營運及任務排程結果，結合即時場域資訊及車輛
  狀態產生巡航控制決策與指令，**不負責營運策略制定**。

- Vehicle Traffic Management (incl. Vehicle Monitoring System): responsible for formulating
  operating strategies such as fleet operation management, mission scheduling, dispatch planning
  and speed limits, and for delivering the resulting strategies to the Cruise Management System
  (Center Side); **not responsible for cruise control logic**.
- Vehicle Cruise Management System (Center Side): responsible for receiving and interpreting the
  operational and mission scheduling results, and for generating cruise control decisions and
  commands in combination with real-time field conditions and vehicle status; **not responsible for
  the formulation of operating strategies**.

**IOT SCADA 監控管理系統 IOT SCADA Monitoring and Management System**

- 車輛行車管理（含車輛監控系統）：接收 IOT SCADA 提供之場域設施狀態與告警資訊，作為圖台
  顯示、事件管理及營運判斷依據；並提供車輛預估到站（ETA）資訊予 IOT SCADA 使用，
  **不負責場域設施之控制**。
- IOT SCADA 監控管理系統：負責場域設施狀態蒐集、告警管理及設備管理，**不負責營運班表與
  調度決策**。

- Vehicle Traffic Management (incl. Vehicle Monitoring System): receives field facility status and
  alarm information provided by the IOT SCADA system as the basis for dashboard display, event
  management and operational judgement, and provides vehicle estimated time of arrival (ETA)
  information to the IOT SCADA system; **not responsible for the control of field facilities**.
- IOT SCADA Monitoring and Management System: responsible for the collection of field facility
  status, alarm management and equipment management; **not responsible for operational schedules
  or dispatch decisions**.

**自動駕駛系統與 V2X 系統 Autonomous Driving System and V2X System**

- 車輛行車管理（含車輛監控系統）：**不直接介接**自動駕駛系統與 V2X 系統；所需之車輛運行
  狀態、感知資訊及事件資訊，均經由車輛巡航管理系統（中心端）彙整後取得。

- Vehicle Traffic Management (incl. Vehicle Monitoring System): **does not interface directly** with
  the Autonomous Driving System or the V2X System. The required vehicle operating status,
  perception information and event information are obtained after aggregation by the Vehicle Cruise
  Management System (Center Side).

**設備／車輛維護管理系統 Equipment / Vehicle Maintenance Management System**

- 車輛行車管理（含車輛監控系統）：負責整備任務之設定及其於營運班表中之時段安排，
  **不負責維修工作安排、維修記錄、維修文件管理及財產庫存管理**。

- Vehicle Traffic Management (incl. Vehicle Monitoring System): responsible for the configuration of
  maintenance tasks and for arranging their time slots within the operational schedule; **not
  responsible for maintenance work scheduling, maintenance records, maintenance document
  management or property inventory management**.

## 軟體分層設計 Software Layered Design

車輛行車管理（含車輛監控系統）軟體共分為四層，各層亦劃分不同功能模組，功能模組與其子功能
職責細部說明請參閱第 02584K 章 車輛行車管理系統 2.2.3 說明。

The software of the Vehicle Traffic Management System (incl. Vehicle Monitoring System) is divided
into four layers, each further divided into functional modules. For detailed descriptions of the
functional modules and the responsibilities of their sub-functions, please refer to Section 02584K,
Vehicle Traffic Management System, Clause 2.2.3.

**營運核心層 Operation Core Layer**

- 數據監控模組 Data Monitoring Module
- 營運管理模組 Operation Management Module
- 班表管理模組 Schedule Management Module
- 事件管理模組 Event Management Module

**資源配置層 Resource Config Layer**

- 載具管理模組 Vehicle Management Module
- 場域管理模組 Field Management Module
- 服務管理模組 Service Management Module
- 媒體管理模組 Media Management Module

**資通訊安全層 Communication Security Layer**

- 資訊通訊模組 Access Management Module

**後勤管理層 Logistics Mangament Layer**

- 權限管理模組 Access Management Module
- 日誌管理模組 Field Management Module
- 系統基礎模組 System Foundation Module
